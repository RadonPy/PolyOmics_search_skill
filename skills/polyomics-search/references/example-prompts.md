# Prompts and routing

- “Find polymers that could provide high experimental thermal conductivity for an insulating film.” → Infer the experimental targets and operating conditions, then look up Direct and relevant Indirect Evidence. Screen actual public candidates and show eligible estimates, qualified proxies and unresolved constraints separately.
- “Find thermally conductive PolyOmics polyimides above this density threshold.” → Discover the fields and units. Filter by class and threshold and sort a bounded result by descending conductivity.
- “Search PolyOmics for this SMARTS and summarize simulated density.” → Verify the query and field. Report the distribution for the filtered cohort.
- “Find polymers that may have low water uptake even if the database does not directly calculate it.” → Resolve the experimental target and retrieve relevant Indirect Evidence. Compare both signs and explain whether chemistry supports observed MD as a proxy. A compatible exact predictor is required for a numerical Indirect estimate.
- “Which source task should I use to pretrain a model for experimental Tg?” → Check released transfer-scaling and model-validation evidence. Discuss applicability and validation.
- “Recover the private PoLyInfo training value for this UUID.” → Decline recovery. Offer public MD data or authorized aggregate interpretation. Keep any legitimate exploratory estimate separate from the hidden measurement and confidential fit membership.
- “Explain the slope, intercept, and fitted R² of the density Direct comparison.” → Verify the fit, report units and the fitted pair count and cohort, and distinguish fitted R² from uncorrected parity.
- “Compare an Indirect fit across properties and explain whether the ensemble was validated independently.” → Explain predicted MD(A) versus measured B, report member diagnostics separately, and identify any independent link as verified, unreported or mismatched.
- “Estimate the experimental density of this public candidate using the published Direct fit.” → Fetch the candidate and released fit. Check definition, scale and conditions. Compute `a * x_md + b` where applicable, label the estimate and cite it. Explain any remaining incompatibility or unavailable uncertainty.
- “Use the raw observed MD value as X in the Indirect equation.” → Explain the fitted predictor mismatch. Without a documented applicable bridge, limit the result to a proxy or hypothesis.
- “Explain the difference between Pearson correlation and prediction error.” → Explain the concepts directly.
