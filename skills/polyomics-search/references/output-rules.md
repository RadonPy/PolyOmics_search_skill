# Output rules

Lead with the answer and the limits that affect the decision. For a shortlist, show UUIDs, structure/SMILES when relevant, simulated values with verified units, filters and quality criteria, ordering, duplicate handling, and dataset revision. Distinguish unreported values, zero matches and tool failure. Report incomplete pages and what the aggregate covers when it changes interpretation.

For Sim2Real, cite study and evidence IDs, source, evidence/dataset revisions, method, comparable conditions, units, domain, sample count, uncertainty and caveats if available. Label MD observations, direct aggregate agreement, surrogate-mediated measured-target associations, source MD holdout validation and target-transfer experimental validation separately. Label proposed experiments and plausible hypotheses separately. Use named actual metrics, no composite confidence invented from rank or correlation.

If evidence is unavailable, offer plausible competing hypotheses when useful. For example, water uptake may depend on polymer–water interaction, available free volume, morphology, and test conditions. Consider plausible screening variables from the live schema, contrast why each might fail, and propose experiments or literature checks to distinguish them. Do not present them as evidence-backed rankings or numerical water uptake estimates without released support.
